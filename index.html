Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains how Expense Tracker handles information when you use the Android app. Questions can be sent to littlesteps.surat@gmail.com.

What the app does

Expense Tracker helps you record expenses, income, transfers, accounts, categories, payees, notes, receipts, and related personal finance information. It can also create local backups and reports. The app does not provide banking, investment, tax, accounting, or other financial advice.

Information stored on your device

Your financial records are stored in the app's private storage on your device. This may include:

  • Accounts, balances, transactions, transfers, categories, payment methods, tags, budgets, and related settings
  • Payee or payer names, notes, dates, reference details, and other information you enter
  • Receipt images or files you choose to attach
  • App preferences and optional app-lock settings

LittleSteps Lab does not operate a server for your financial records, does not require an account, and does not receive this locally stored information.

Backups, imports, reports, and sharing

When you create a backup, the app writes a database file and receipt files to a folder you select. When you import a backup or another compatible database, the app reads the folder or file you select. These actions happen on your device.

When you export or share a report, you choose the receiving app or service. Information you send is then handled under that recipient's privacy practices. LittleSteps Lab does not receive a copy.

Optional device features

  • Phone lock or biometrics: Android performs authentication. Expense Tracker receives only the success or failure result and does not receive or store your biometric data.
  • Receipts: You may choose an existing file or use a camera app to provide a receipt. The resulting file is stored locally by Expense Tracker.
  • Local desktop access: If you enable this feature, another device on your local network may access the information shown by the app. The feature does not send your financial records to a LittleSteps Lab server.
  • Notifications: If a feature requests notification permission, you can allow or deny it in Android settings.

Advertising and consent

Expense Tracker displays banner ads supplied by Google AdMob. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process device and usage information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, device and app details, diagnostics, and ad interactions to provide, secure, and measure advertising.

Your financial records and receipt files are not sent to Google AdMob by Expense Tracker. Where required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to present advertising privacy choices. When available, you can reopen those choices from the app. Learn more from Google's explanation of how it uses information from apps that use its services.

Permissions

The app uses internet and network access for advertising and optional local desktop access. It may request notification permission for notification-based features and biometric permission for optional phone-lock authentication. File and folder access is requested only through Android's system picker when you choose a receipt, backup, import, or export location.

Sharing and sale of information

LittleSteps Lab does not sell your personal or financial information. Data may be processed by Google through the advertising service described above, or by another app or service when you explicitly choose to share or export information to it.

Your choices and data deletion

  • Edit or delete transactions and other records using the app's available controls.
  • Delete receipt files by removing their associated records where supported.
  • Delete local app data through Android settings or uninstall the app.
  • Delete backup folders and exported reports from the locations where you saved them.
  • Manage advertising privacy choices in the app when the option is available, and manage or reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

Uninstalling the app does not delete backups or reports you saved outside the app's private storage.

Data security and retention

Financial records remain on your device until you delete them, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Backups and exports remain in the locations you selected until you delete them. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so you are responsible for protecting your device and saved backup files.

Children

Expense Tracker is a general-audience personal finance utility and is not directed to children under 13. LittleSteps Lab does not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated server.

Changes to this policy

If the app or its privacy practices change, this page will be updated with a new date. The Play Store Data safety information will also be kept consistent with the app and this policy.
